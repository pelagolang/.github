# Code of Conduct

The Pelago community follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in full. The short version:

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; assume good faith.
- Keep discussion on topic for the project.

## Scope

This applies in every repository, issue, pull request, discussion and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers: open a [private security advisory](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories) on any repository of the organization (use it for conduct reports too, mark the title "Conduct"), or message [@C-EO](https://github.com/C-EO) on GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
